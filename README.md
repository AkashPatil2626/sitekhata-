  <h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
  <p class="date">SiteKhata · Last updated: 21 October 2026</p>

  <p>This policy explains how the SiteKhata Android app ("the app") handles information. The app is published by Akash Patil ("we").</p>

  <h2>Summary</h2>
  <p>The app stores your data only on your phone. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any of it. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.</p>

  <h2>Information you enter</h2>
  <p>You may enter worker names, phone numbers, work types, daily wages, construction site details (name, location, client name and contact number), attendance records, material purchases (material, quantity, rate, amount, supplier name and bill number) and notes. This information is saved in a private database inside the app on your device. It is never sent to us or to any server.</p>

  <h2>Backups you create</h2>
  <p>When you tap Backup, the app creates a backup file and opens your phone's share menu. The file goes only where you choose to send it (for example your Google Drive, WhatsApp or email). Those services' own privacy policies apply to files you send to them. Keep backup files private, as they contain your records.</p>
  <p>If Android's own device backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup. This is controlled by you in your phone's settings.</p>

  <h2>Purchases</h2>
  <p>The optional "Pro – Lifetime" upgrade is bought through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we do not receive your card, UPI or bank details. The app only checks with Google Play whether your Google account owns the upgrade. See Google's privacy policy at <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy">policies.google.com/privacy</a>.</p>

  <h2>Internet access</h2>
  <p>The app uses the internet only to talk to Google Play for the Pro purchase and restore. All other features work offline.</p>

  <h2>Permissions</h2>
  <p>The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. When you restore a backup, you pick the file yourself using Android's file picker.</p>

  <h2>Deleting your data</h2>
  <p>You are in full control. Archive or edit records in the app, or delete everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage (Settings → Apps → SiteKhata → Storage → Clear storage). Backup files you shared must be deleted from wherever you saved them.</p>

  <h2>Children</h2>
  <p>The app is intended for adults running construction work and is not directed at children.</p>

  <h2>Changes</h2>
  <p>If we change how the app handles information (for example by adding cloud sync), we will update this page and the date above before releasing that version.</p>

  <h2>Contact</h2>
  <p>Questions? Email <a href="mailto:sitekhata@gmail.com">sitekhata@gmail.com</a>.</p>
